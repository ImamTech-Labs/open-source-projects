# Community Guidelines

ImamTech Labs welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Our goal is to make collaboration useful, respectful, and accessible.

## How we work together

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and technical disagreements without personal attacks.
- Ask questions when requirements or feedback are unclear.
- Give feedback about the work, not about the person.
- Respect other contributors' time and acknowledge their effort.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and welcoming to newcomers.

## When contributing

- Check existing issues and pull requests before starting work.
- Explain your proposal before making large or breaking changes.
- Share enough context for others to understand and review your work.
- Do not post passwords, access tokens, private keys, or personal information.
- Follow each repository's contribution and security instructions.

## Maintaining a welcoming space

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, insults, and deliberately disruptive behaviour are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the community constructive.

## Raising a concern

If a discussion becomes disrespectful or you have a concern about community behaviour, contact the repository maintainers privately through the GitHub organization rather than escalating the disagreement publicly.

These guidelines apply to issues, pull requests, code reviews, and other project spaces maintained by ImamTech Labs.
